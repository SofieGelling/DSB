Table Employees
-----------------
Col 1: File row number
Col 2: Employee's Full Name (, title if known)
Col 3: Internal Employee Number
Col 4: Gender
Col 5: Residence of Employee
Col 6: Date of Birth
Col 7: Annual Salary (in euros, based on 1FTE)
Col 8: Employee's Work Location
Col 9: Contract Start Date / Employee since
Col 10: Contract End Date / Employee until (when empty the employee has a contract of indefinite duration)
Col 11: Consultancy grade (if consultant)
Col 12: Contract in FTE (1.0FTE is 40 hours/wk and 25 vacation days)
